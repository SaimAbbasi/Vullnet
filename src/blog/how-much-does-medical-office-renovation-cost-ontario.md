## What Drives the Cost of a Medical Office Renovation

### HVAC and Mechanical

Medical offices require more mechanical work than standard offices. Exam rooms need individual temperature control. Procedure rooms may require dedicated exhaust. Dental operatories need compressed air lines and vacuum systems. HVAC typically accounts for 20-30% of the total renovation budget in a medical space.

### Infection Control (ICRA)

If you are renovating in or adjacent to an active healthcare facility, ICRA (Infection Control Risk Assessment) protocols add cost. Temporary barriers, negative air pressure systems, HEPA filtration, and specific sequencing requirements can add $5-15 per square foot depending on the risk classification.

### Plumbing

Medical offices have significantly more plumbing than typical commercial spaces. Hand-wash stations in every exam room, sterilization areas, lab sinks, and accessible washrooms all add to the plumbing scope. Budget $15-30 per square foot for plumbing in a medical build-out, compared to $5-10 in a standard office.

### Electrical and Data

Modern medical offices need robust electrical infrastructure for diagnostic equipment, imaging systems, and electronic health record workstations at every point of care. A typical 3,000 sq ft medical suite may have 40-60 data drops compared to 15-25 in a standard office.

### Permit Costs and Timelines

Medical office renovations in Toronto typically take 8-12 weeks for permit approval. Mississauga is similar at 8-12 weeks. Permit fees in Toronto are calculated based on construction value and typically run $3,000-8,000 for a medical suite renovation.
